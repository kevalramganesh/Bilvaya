About Bilvaya

A small studio, a long view.

Bilvaya was founded in 2016 by a designer, a chef, and an operator who had spent a decade wondering why so much of Indian hospitality felt copied from somewhere else. We started with a single coffee bar in Bengaluru and grew, slowly, from there.

Our approach

We are not a hotel group.

We are a studio that happens to run hotels, and cafes, and residences, and restaurants. Every project we take on is designed in-house, run by our own people, and held against the same short list of questions. Does the room feel considered? Does the food feel personal? Would we send our closest friends here?

If the answer to any of those is no, we do not open. The portfolio grows one property at a time, and we would rather stay small for longer than compromise on any of the parts that make hospitality feel like hospitality.

How we work

Six things we believe, quietly.

Not a manifesto. Just the operating notes we keep pinned to the studio wall, and the reasons we tend to say no to more projects than we say yes to.

01

Design is a shared draft

Architects, chefs, housekeepers, and general managers sit at the same table from week one. The brief belongs to all of them.

02

Buy less, buy better

Every specification passes a simple test: will it look better in ten years than it did on day one? If not, we look again.

03

Local, actually

Local craft, local staff, local produce, local suppliers. Not a story on the website, a line item in the budget.

04

Operations is the design

A beautiful room that runs badly is a bad room. Half our design reviews are about how the housekeeping trolley turns a corner.

05

Slow openings

We soft-open every property for six weeks before the doors are properly open. Guests forgive a delay. They rarely forgive a rushed launch.

06

Say the number

We are honest about margins with our partners. Hospitality is a patient business, and no one benefits from a pitch that pretends otherwise.
