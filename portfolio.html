Portfolio

Fourteen places, four disciplines.

A working list of the properties we design, build, and operate. Each project starts with a site and a question, what does this room want to become?

01 · Resorts

Resorts & Retreats

Boutique properties built for the long weekend and the slower week. Twelve to forty keys, generous verandahs, and kitchens with a point of view.

Aranya, Coorg

A 22-key hillside retreat above the Kaveri, built around a single banyan, three spring-fed pools, and a working coffee estate.

  • 22 keys
  • Open 2019
  • Karnataka

Kavya House, Alleppey

An eight-room heritage villa on a private stretch of backwater, restored in lime plaster, rosewood, and hand-thrown terracotta tile.

  • 8 keys
  • Open 2021
  • Kerala
02 · Cafes

Cafes & Bakeries

Neighbourhood rooms built around a single espresso bar, a daily bread programme, and a set of chairs worth staying an hour in.

Terra & Co., Bengaluru

Speciality coffee, sourdough, and slow breakfasts in a quiet lane off 12th Main. Beans roasted weekly in-house.

  • 42 seats
  • Open 2020
  • Indiranagar

Terra & Co., Kochi

A second Terra, in a restored spice warehouse in Mattancherry, with a wood-fired oven and a small library upstairs.

  • 36 seats
  • Open 2023
  • Fort Kochi
03 · Residences

Student Residences

Purpose-built homes for students, safe, social, and dignified. Twin and single rooms, three daily meals, a proper library, and rooftops made for staying up late.

Study Hall, Pune

A 180-bed residence next to Symbiosis, with a fifty-seat dining hall, a two-storey library, and a rooftop garden overlooking the hills.

  • 180 beds
  • Open 2022
  • Lavale

Study Hall, Bengaluru

A 240-bed residence in Whitefield with a music room, laundry service, and a common kitchen for the students who cook.

  • 240 beds
  • Open 2024
  • Whitefield
04 · Restaurants

Restaurants & Bars

Rooms with a clear point of view. Chef-led kitchens, seasonal menus, and a bar that pours with intention rather than volume.

Firewood, Mumbai

A twelve-seat chef counter, a wood-fired hearth, and a menu that changes with the market. Reservations open on the 1st of each month.

  • 28 seats
  • Open 2022
  • Bandra West

Kaveri, Coorg

The all-day restaurant at Aranya, open to non-residents. A short seasonal menu built almost entirely from within a fifty-kilometre radius.

  • 60 seats
  • Open 2019
  • At Aranya
