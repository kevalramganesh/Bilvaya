About Bilvaya Hospitality

We are a hospitality group building a multi brand portfolio across resorts, hotels and student focused living. Our approach blends data driven operations with a simple promise: make every stay smooth, safe and memorable.

Bilvaya Hospitality works with owners, investors and institutions to unlock the full potential of their assets. We focus on clear brand positioning, disciplined cost control and service standards that guests notice.

Our teams handle everything from concept and design to day to day operations, sales and revenue management. This allows property owners to benefit from professional management while we focus on long term performance.

With a presence across key demand centers, we are building a platform that can scale without losing the personal touch that defines good hospitality.

Student hospitality

We treat student housing as a core hospitality vertical, not an afterthought. Our properties are designed to support academic success, safety and a sense of community.

How we approach student living

Bilvaya Student Living combines secure, well maintained accommodation with services that matter to students and parents. We work with educational institutions, developers and investors to create housing that is both affordable and well run.

Our model focuses on location, safety, reliable utilities and responsive on ground teams. We also invest in common areas that encourage peer learning and healthy routines.

Key features

  • Secure access with controlled entry and on site staff
  • Furnished rooms with study tables, storage and high speed internet
  • Common kitchens, lounges and laundry facilities
  • Regular housekeeping and preventive maintenance
  • Community events, wellness activities and peer support

Our values
